The sprite is 64 patches of 3×3 pixels, each picked from a learned dictionary of 1,024. The model picks them one at a time; every step is one more row of the same formula grid, so a single recalculation draws the whole sprite.

What we learned, and what's next.

Exactness is cheap; quality is hard. With a float64 spec and parity gates, the sheet matched from day one. Making the drawings good took 17 rounds.

Look at the pictures. Blobs come from averaging. Hard patches plus guidance fixed them; the loss never told us.

Speed follows formula count. A cheaper guidance chain (61k formulas instead of ~180k) is what got guidance onto the sheet.

Next time: cut latency below a minute, and fix weak creatures like white cats.
